Criteria

Pixel size = 25.4 ÷ scan DPI (72 dpi → 0.353 mm, 150 dpi → 0.169 mm). The target only colours the result; it is not a clinical standard.

Result

–

Gamma index per pixel: 1 or below passes. White areas fall under the low-dose threshold (a percentage of the reference maximum) and are not evaluated.
